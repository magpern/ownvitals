ownvitals UI design for M6 · chosen by the owner, 31 Oct 2026

Quiet instrument

A calm, precise instrument for reading your own body data. Every number sits on your own history, says who measured or computed it, and admits what is missing.

The logo is the idea in miniature: a reading (the dot) on your personal typical range (the band).

Six principles
01

Your history is the reference

Every series is drawn against your own typical range. Comparisons are with your past, never with population charts.

Trend tier, PLAN §7.2

02

Gaps stay gaps

Missing days are hatched and lines break. A stream we can't read is never a zero and never "normal".

Capabilities, §4.2

03

Say who said it

Withings-reported values, ownvitals calculations and experimental estimates each carry their own mark. An estimate never looks certified.

D9, §7.3

04

Freshness on every screen

Each page opens with when you last measured, when we last synced, and which imports are running, incomplete or broken.

MVP UI rule, §8

05

Every number is traceable

Overview → metric → day → reading → how it was computed: method, algorithm version, inputs and data revision.

Provenance, §5

06

Quiet by default

No streaks, rings, confetti or nudges. Colour appears only when something deserves a look. An analysis tool, not a copy of the Withings app.

Withings terms, §9.4

The signature chart

One chart grammar for every metric: your readings on your typical range

  • Typical range. Your rolling median ± 1.5 MAD over 90 days, by time, not by point count.
  • Smoothed line. Gap-weighted EWMA. It stops where the data stops.
  • Daily value. First morning weigh-in (04:00–11:00), otherwise the median of the day.
  • Unconfirmed. The scale couldn't tell who stepped on it. Shown, never used.
  • No data. Nothing is drawn across a gap, and it is labelled with its length.
  • Change point. Shown only on series that pass the minimum-data policy.
  • Latest. Bigger, labelled. Orange when outside your typical range, never red.

Finding tiers
Device

Withings' own result, attributed: "Withings classified this ECG as …". Everyone.

Trend

Compared with your own history: "higher than your 90-day typical range". No diagnostic labels. Everyone.

Experimental

Clinical bands and our estimates. Dashed everywhere it appears. Owner flag only, household only, until the M8 review.

System

About your data, not your body: no weigh-in for 14 days, low battery, reconnect needed, import incomplete.
